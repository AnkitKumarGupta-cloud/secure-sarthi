Overview

Jeevan Azad is a non-participating endowment plan, meaning your maturity amount is fixed and guaranteed from day one — it doesn't depend on LIC's bonus declarations. Premiums are paid for a limited period (Policy Term minus 8 years), and cover is capped at a relatively modest ₹5 lakh, making this a smaller-ticket, short-commitment savings plan rather than a primary protection tool.

Key Benefits

  • Guaranteed Maturity Benefit — the payout equals the Basic Sum Assured, fixed at the outset, with no dependency on bonus performance.
  • Short premium-paying window: you pay for the policy term minus 8 years (for example, a 20-year policy needs only 12 years of premiums).
  • No medical examination required for sums assured up to ₹3,00,000; a simplified video-medical process for ₹3,00,000–₹5,00,000.
  • Option to receive the maturity or death benefit in instalments rather than a single lump sum.

Eligibility

  • Entry age: 90 days to 50 years.
  • Maximum age at maturity: 70 years.
  • Sum assured: ₹2,00,000 to ₹5,00,000 per life (combined across all Jeevan Azad policies you hold).
  • Policy term: 15 or 20 years, with premiums paid for the term minus 8 years.

Why Choose This Plan

Best suited to someone who wants a small, guaranteed, non-market-linked savings pot with a short premium commitment — not a primary insurance plan, given the ₹5 lakh cap on cover. Often used alongside a separate term plan rather than instead of one.
