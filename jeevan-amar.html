Overview

LIC New Jeevan Amar is a pure term insurance plan — it pays your family a lump sum if you pass away during the policy term. There's no maturity or survival payout, which is exactly why it's able to offer very high cover for a low premium. You choose between Level Sum Assured (cover stays flat throughout) or Increasing Sum Assured (cover rises automatically to help keep pace with inflation and growing responsibilities).

Key Benefits

  • Choice of Level Sum Assured (fixed cover) or Increasing Sum Assured, which rises 10% a year from year 6 to year 15, then settles at double the original cover from year 16 onward.
  • Flexible premium payment — pay regularly for the full term, over a limited number of years, or as a single one-time premium.
  • Option to receive the death benefit as a lump sum or in monthly instalments, whichever works better for your family.
  • Lower premium rates for women, and rebates for higher sums assured.
  • Optional Accident Benefit Rider for additional payout in case of accidental death.

Eligibility

  • Entry age: 18 to 65 years.
  • Maximum age at maturity: 80 years.
  • Minimum sum assured: ₹25,00,000, with no upper limit (subject to underwriting).
  • Policy term: 10 to 40 years, with premium payment matching the term, a limited-pay option, or a single premium.

Why Choose This Plan

If your main goal is maximum protection for your family at the lowest possible cost — covering a home loan, replacing your income, or securing your children's future — this is one of LIC's most efficient ways to do it. The Increasing Sum Assured option is worth a close look if you're early in your career and expect your responsibilities (and income) to grow over the next decade.
