LIC New Jeevan Shanti

Plan 858 · Pension

A deferred annuity plan — invest a lump sum now, and choose a future date when your pension income starts. Suitable for those 10–20 years away from retirement.

Ask About This Plan → Back to All Plans

Overview

New Jeevan Shanti lets you lock in today's annuity rate for a pension that starts later. You pay a single lump sum now and choose a deferment period of 1 to 12 years before payouts begin — useful if retirement is still some years away but you want to secure a guaranteed rate before it potentially changes.

Key Benefits

  • Deferment period of 1 to 12 years, your choice — the pension income starts only once this period ends.
  • Annuity rate is locked in and guaranteed at the time of purchase, protecting you from future rate declines.
  • Can be purchased for a single life or jointly for two lives, so a spouse's pension continues after the primary annuitant.
  • Single premium only — no ongoing payments to manage after the initial purchase.

Eligibility

  • Entry age: 30 to 79 years.
  • Minimum purchase price: ₹1,50,000, with no upper limit.
  • Premium: single premium only.
  • Deferment period: 1 to 12 years, chosen at purchase.

Why Choose This Plan

A strong fit if you're roughly 10 to 20 years from retirement and want to secure today's annuity rate now, rather than wait and risk purchasing a pension later at a less favourable rate.
