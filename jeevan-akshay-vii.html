Overview

Jeevan Akshay VII is LIC's flagship immediate annuity plan, offering the widest choice of payout structures — ten different annuity options in total, from a simple level-for-life pension to versions that return your purchase price to your nominee or continue paying a spouse after you. You pay once, and your income begins right away at a rate that's locked in for life.

Key Benefits

  • Ten annuity options to choose from — including level annuity for life, annuity with return of purchase price on death, and joint-life options covering a spouse.
  • Annuity rate is guaranteed and locked in at the time of purchase — it never changes for the rest of your life.
  • Payout frequency of your choice: monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, or yearly.
  • Income starts immediately after the single premium is paid — no deferment period.
  • Wide entry-age window and one option (Option F) extending eligibility up to age 100, useful for annuitising very late in life.

Eligibility

  • Entry age: 25 to 85 years for most annuity options (Option J starts from 40; Option F extends up to 100).
  • Minimum purchase price: ₹1,00,000, subject to a minimum resulting annuity amount, with no upper limit.
  • Premium: single premium only.

Why Choose This Plan

Because it offers ten distinct payout structures, this is the plan to look at if you want to fine-tune exactly how your pension behaves — for example, prioritising return of capital to your family over a slightly higher monthly amount, or vice versa.
