Child Plans

Support their dreams, secure their future — payouts timed to education milestones, with your child's plan protected even if you're not around to fund it.

Child's Future LIC New Children's Money Back (Plan 932)

Provides payouts at key stages of your child's life (education milestones) along with a lump sum at maturity, with premium waiver benefit if the parent passes away during the term.

 Child's Future LIC Jeevan Tarun (Plan 934)

Flexible payout options between ages 20–24 for the child, designed around education and early career expenses.

 Child's Future LIC Amritbaal (Plan 874)

A short premium-paying child savings plan with guaranteed additions each year, letting you choose your child's maturity age from 18 to 25.
ⓘ

Premium waiver matters here

Most child plans include a built-in benefit that waives future premiums if the parent passes away — the plan continues and still pays out for the child. I'll confirm this applies before you buy.
