LIC Amritbaal

Plan 874 · Child Plan

A non-linked savings plan for children under 13. Offers guaranteed additions throughout the term and a lump-sum payout at maturity to fund education or other goals.

Ask About This Plan → Back to All Plans

Overview

Amritbaal is LIC's newer child plan, designed for a short premium commitment (as little as 5 years) while your child's fund keeps growing through guaranteed additions until the maturity date you choose. It's aimed at parents who want certainty in the payout rather than a bonus-dependent number, and who'd rather finish paying premiums early.

Key Benefits

  • Guaranteed Addition of ₹80 per ₹1,000 of Sum Assured, added to the policy every year — a fixed, predictable build-up rather than a bonus-linked one.
  • Short premium-paying term: just 5, 6, or 7 years, regardless of how long the policy runs before maturity.
  • Flexible maturity age — choose anywhere from 18 to 25 years old for your child, matching when you expect to need the funds.
  • Premium Waiver Benefit if the parent (proposer) dies during the term, keeping the policy and its payout on track.

Eligibility

  • Child's entry age: 30 days to 13 years.
  • Maturity age: selectable from 18 to 25 years.
  • Premium paying term: 5, 6, or 7 years.

Why Choose This Plan

A good option if you'd rather finish your premium commitment quickly — within 5 to 7 years — while still choosing exactly when the policy pays out based on your child's age, and you prefer a guaranteed build-up over a bonus-dependent one.
