LIC New Children's Money Back

Plan 932 · Child Plan

Pays at key milestones in your child's life — ages 18, 20, and 22 — plus a lump sum at maturity. If the parent dies during the term, all future premiums are waived and the policy remains in force.

Ask About This Plan → Back to All Plans

Overview

This plan is built around your child's real milestones — it pays out at ages 18, 20, and 22, when education and early career expenses tend to land, plus a final lump sum at maturity (age 25). If something happens to the parent during the term, all future premiums are waived automatically and the policy continues in full force, so your child's payouts are protected either way.

Key Benefits

  • Survival Benefits paid when your child turns 18, 20, and 22 — timed to help with higher-secondary, undergraduate, and postgraduate stages.
  • Maturity Benefit: A further lump sum, plus bonuses, paid when your child turns 25.
  • Premium Waiver Benefit: If the parent (proposer) passes away during the term, all remaining premiums are waived, and the policy — along with all future payouts — continues as planned.
  • Can be taken out by a parent or grandparent on behalf of a child aged 0 to 12.

Eligibility

  • Child's entry age: 0 to 12 years.
  • Maturity age: 25 years (fixed).
  • Policy term / premium paying term: 25 minus the child's age at entry.
  • Minimum sum assured: ₹1,00,000, with no upper limit.

Why Choose This Plan

Well suited if you want payouts that land close to real expenses — school-leaving, college, postgraduate study — rather than one distant lump sum, combined with the reassurance that the premium waiver keeps the plan on track even if you're not there to keep paying.
