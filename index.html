LIC Plans

Plans I can help you with.

A general overview of LIC's major plan categories. Exact premiums depend on your age, sum assured, and policy term — I'll calculate this with you directly.

Term Insurance

Pure Protection LIC New Tech Term (Plan 954)

High life cover at low premium, paid entirely to your family in case of death during the policy term. No maturity benefit — built purely for protection.

Pure Protection LIC New Jeevan Amar (Plan 955)

Flexible term plan with level or increasing cover options, designed to protect your family's financial future at an affordable premium.

Pure Protection LIC Saral Jeevan Bima (Plan 859)

A standard, simplified term plan with straightforward terms, making it easy to compare across insurers.

Endowment & Savings Plans

Savings + Protection LIC New Jeevan Anand (Plan 715)

Combines life cover with guaranteed savings — pays a maturity benefit if you survive the term, plus continued life cover even after maturity.

Savings + Protection LIC Jeevan Labh (Plan 936)

Limited premium payment with a longer cover period, popular for its balance of guaranteed returns and life protection.

Savings + Protection LIC New Endowment (Plan 714)

A traditional participating plan combining savings and protection, with bonuses added throughout the policy term.

Money-Back Plans

Periodic Payout LIC New Money Back – 20 Years (Plan 920)

Pays a percentage of the sum assured at regular intervals during the policy term, while keeping full life cover active throughout.

Periodic Payout LIC New Money Back – 25 Years (Plan 921)

Similar structure over a longer term, useful for those planning around specific future milestones like a child's education.

Child Plans

Child's Future LIC New Children's Money Back (Plan 932)

Provides payouts at key stages of your child's life (education milestones) along with a lump sum at maturity, with premium waiver benefit if the parent passes away during the term.

Child's Future LIC Jeevan Tarun (Plan 934)

Flexible payout options between ages 20–24 for the child, designed around education and early career expenses.

Whole Life & Pension Plans

Whole Life LIC Jeevan Umang (Plan 745)

Pays a yearly survival benefit after the premium paying term ends, plus a lump sum and life cover up to age 100.

Retirement LIC Saral Pension

A single-premium immediate annuity plan providing guaranteed regular pension income for life after retirement.

Retirement LIC New Jeevan Shanti

A deferred annuity plan, letting you build a pension corpus that starts paying out at a future date you choose.
