LIC New Money Back – 25 Years

Plan 921 · Money-Back

Similar periodic payout structure over a longer 25-year term. Ideal for planning around longer-horizon milestones like a child's higher education or wedding expenses.

Overview

The 25-year sibling of LIC's Money Back range, structured around four periodic payouts instead of three. It suits longer-horizon planning — a child's higher education, a wedding years away — while keeping full life cover active for the entire term.

Key Benefits

  • Survival Benefit: 15% of Sum Assured paid at the end of the 5th, 10th, 15th, and 20th policy years — four payouts before maturity.
  • Maturity Benefit: The remaining 40% of Sum Assured plus vested bonuses and Final Additional Bonus, paid at the end of the 25th year.
  • Death Benefit: Full Sum Assured plus bonuses paid to your nominee if death occurs during the term — unreduced by survival payouts already received.
  • Life cover remains active for the full 25 years regardless of payouts already collected.

Eligibility

  • Entry age: 13 to 45 years.
  • Maximum age at maturity: 70 years.
  • Minimum sum assured: ₹1,00,000, with no upper limit.
  • Policy term: 25 years (fixed), with premiums paid over 20 years.

Why Choose This Plan

A good fit if you're planning around a longer horizon — for example, starting this policy when your child is young, with payouts timed to land around key milestones through their schooling and into early adulthood.
