LIC New Money Back – 20 Years

Plan 920 · Money-Back

Pays survival benefits at the 5th, 10th, and 15th year, with a final payout plus bonuses at maturity. Full life cover is maintained throughout the 20-year term.

Ask About This Plan → Back to All Plans

Overview

This plan pays out in stages rather than making you wait until maturity for everything. Over the 20-year term you receive three survival payouts along the way, a final payout with bonuses at the end, and your full life cover stays active the entire time — even after you've already received money back.

Key Benefits

  • Survival Benefit: 20% of Sum Assured paid at the end of the 5th, 10th, and 15th policy years — three payouts before maturity.
  • Maturity Benefit: The remaining 40% of Sum Assured plus vested bonuses and Final Additional Bonus, paid at the end of the 20th year.
  • Death Benefit: If you pass away during the term, your nominee receives the full Sum Assured plus bonuses — the survival payouts already received do not reduce this amount.
  • Full life cover remains active for the entire 20-year term, regardless of how many survival benefits you've already collected.

Eligibility

  • Entry age: 13 to 50 years.
  • Maximum age at maturity: 70 years.
  • Minimum sum assured: ₹1,00,000, with no upper limit.
  • Policy term: 20 years (fixed), with premiums paid over 15 years.

Why Choose This Plan

Useful if you have periodic expenses you can anticipate over the next two decades — school fees, a planned purchase, or simply wanting liquidity at intervals rather than one distant maturity date — while still keeping full life cover in place throughout.
