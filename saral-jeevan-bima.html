Overview

Saral Jeevan Bima is a standardised pure term plan — its structure, wording, and benefits are set by IRDAI and offered in a nearly identical form by every insurer in India, LIC included. That makes it genuinely simple to understand and easy to compare against other companies' Saral Jeevan Bima plans on premium alone, with no hidden variation in terms.

Key Benefits

  • Straightforward death benefit — Sum Assured paid to your nominee if you pass away during the term, no complicated calculations.
  • Flexible premium options: pay a single lump sum, pay regularly for the full term, or pay over a limited period (5 or 10 years).
  • Standardised, plain-language policy wording — the same core structure across insurers makes shopping around genuinely apples-to-apples.
  • Available with yearly, half-yearly, or monthly (NACH) premium payments.

Eligibility

  • Entry age: 18 to 65 years.
  • Maximum age at maturity: 70 years.
  • Sum assured: ₹5,00,000 to ₹25,00,000, in multiples of ₹50,000.
  • Policy term: 5 to 40 years.

Why Choose This Plan

This suits first-time term insurance buyers who want a smaller, simpler cover amount (up to ₹25 lakh) without wading through rider options and fine print — and who value being able to compare LIC's premium directly against other insurers' Saral Jeevan Bima offerings on a like-for-like basis.
