LIC Jeevan Umang

Plan 745 · Whole Life

After the premium payment term ends, LIC pays a yearly survival benefit for the rest of your life — plus a lump-sum maturity or death benefit. Coverage extends to age 100.

Overview

Jeevan Umang combines whole-life protection with an income stream that starts once you're done paying premiums. You choose how long you pay — 15, 20, 25, or 30 years — and from the year after that ends, LIC pays you 8% of your sum assured every single year, for the rest of your life, right up to age 100. At 100 (or on earlier death), the full sum assured plus bonuses is paid out.

Key Benefits

  • Guaranteed Annual Survival Benefit: 8% of Basic Sum Assured, paid every year starting right after your chosen premium-paying term ends, continuing until age 100.
  • Maturity Benefit at age 100: full Sum Assured plus vested bonuses and Final Additional Bonus, paid as a lump sum.
  • Death Benefit at any point before 100: full Sum Assured plus vested bonuses paid to your nominee.
  • You choose the premium-paying term (15, 20, 25, or 30 years) to match your income timeline — pay for a fixed period, then receive income for potentially decades afterward.
  • Premiums qualify for tax benefits under Section 80C; the death benefit is tax-free.

Eligibility

  • Entry age: 90 days to 55 years, depending on the premium-paying term chosen (up to 55 for 15 years, down to 40 for 30 years).
  • Policy term: 100 minus your age at entry (cover runs to age 100).
  • Minimum sum assured: ₹2,00,000, with no upper limit.
  • Premium-paying term: 15, 20, 25, or 30 years, your choice.

Why Choose This Plan

A strong option if you want an income stream that starts once you stop working (or once you stop paying premiums) and keeps paying for the rest of your life, combined with a life cover that never really "ends" the way a term or endowment plan does. Often used for retirement income planning or to build a policy for a child that later becomes their income source.
