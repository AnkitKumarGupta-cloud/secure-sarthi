Overview

Jeevan Tarun is a flexible child plan where you choose, at the time of taking the policy, how the survival benefits are structured — either spread as annual payouts between your child's ages 20 and 24, or as milestone lump sums. Premiums stop once your child turns 20, but the policy itself continues to maturity at 25, and a premium waiver benefit protects the plan if the parent isn't there to keep paying.

Key Benefits

  • Flexible payout structure — you select the option that spreads survival benefits across your child's ages 20 to 24, matched to when education or early-career costs typically arise.
  • Premiums are paid only until your child turns 20, even though the policy continues to maturity at 25.
  • Maturity Benefit: the balance of the sum assured plus vested bonuses, paid when your child turns 25.
  • Premium Waiver Benefit: if the parent or guardian (proposer) dies during the term, future premiums are waived and the policy continues.

Eligibility

  • Child's entry age: 0 to 12 years.
  • Maturity age: 25 years (fixed).
  • Premiums payable until the child reaches age 20.
  • Sum assured is chosen at the proposal stage based on your goals and budget.

Why Choose This Plan

Worth considering if you want more control over exactly how and when your child's payouts land, rather than accepting a fixed milestone schedule — useful if you already have a rough idea of your child's education timeline.
