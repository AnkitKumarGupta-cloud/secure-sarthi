Overview

New Jeevan Anand combines a traditional endowment with lifelong protection. You pay premiums throughout the policy term and receive a guaranteed maturity payout if you survive it — but unlike a typical endowment plan, your life cover doesn't stop there. It continues at the basic sum assured level for the rest of your life, with no further premiums required.

Key Benefits

  • Maturity Benefit: Basic Sum Assured plus vested Simple Reversionary Bonuses and Final Additional Bonus, paid as a lump sum if you survive the policy term.
  • Death Benefit: If you pass away during the term, your nominee receives the sum assured plus bonuses accrued to that point.
  • Whole-life cover after maturity — the basic sum assured continues protecting your family for life, at no extra premium, even decades after the policy has matured.
  • Option to receive the maturity or death benefit in instalments over 5, 10, or 15 years instead of a single lump sum.
  • Premiums qualify for deduction under Section 80C; maturity proceeds are generally tax-free under Section 10(10D).

Eligibility

  • Entry age: 18 to 50 years.
  • Maximum age at maturity: 75 years.
  • Minimum sum assured: ₹2,00,000, with no upper limit.
  • Policy term: 15 to 35 years, with premiums paid throughout.

Why Choose This Plan

If you want a guaranteed lump sum for a future goal — retirement, a child's wedding, paying off a home loan — while also keeping some life cover in place for your entire lifetime, this bridges both needs in one policy. It's a good fit for someone who's already comfortable with the lower, guaranteed-style returns typical of LIC endowment plans and values the "cover continues after maturity" feature specifically.
